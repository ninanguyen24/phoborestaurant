Our Story

Slow-simmered broth, made the way our family has always made it

Pho Bo Restaurant brings the flavors of Vietnamese home cooking to your table — beef bones and warm spices simmered low for hours, fresh herbs picked daily, and noodles served the traditional way. Every bowl is made to order, every recipe passed down rather than shortcut.

Whether you're stopping in for a quick banh mi or settling in for a full bowl of pho, we want it to taste like it was made for you specifically — because it was.
